📓 Castle chronicle

How to play

1

Build

Tap 🧱 Build and pick a stone. Tap the ground to lay it, or drag to lay a whole row. Build walls up, then a floor across the top. Towers, roofs, spires, doors, windows and stairs are all in the Build bar.

2

Rooms

When a space is closed in on every side (walls, a floor and a ceiling) it becomes a room. Tap it with 🏷️ Name and choose what it is for: a throne room, a kitchen, a bedchamber, stables… Each kind of room opens its own page of furniture.

3

Furnish

Tap 🪑 Furnish, pick a room's page and tap things into it. Tapestries go on the walls, chandeliers on the ceiling, beds and tables on the floor. If a thing will not fit, the outline turns red and the castle tells you why.

4

The moat and the drawbridge

Pick the moat spade and tap the ground in front of the castle to dig. Water fills the ditch and swans come to swim. Put a drawbridge over it by the gate: it comes down by day and goes up at night.

5

Life

Name a ground-floor room "Stables" and add stalls: a horse comes to each one. Tap a horse to brush it. Battlements bring knights, who patrol the walls and train at a quintain. A kitchen brings a cook, a library a sage, a forge a smith. People use what you build: they cook, read, sleep and climb your stairs.

6

Days and events

At dusk everyone feasts in the great hall (if it has a long table). Every other day the knights joust along the jousting rail. A royal carriage comes to visit, traders come on market day, and fireworks light the night. Build a tower ten blocks high and a friendly dragon comes to sit on it.

7

Quests and coins

Tap 📜 to see the royal quests. Each one you finish brings coins for the treasury and opens new things. The village sends coins every morning too. Changed your mind? ↶ undoes and ↷ redoes.

📖

I read to build

Pick "I read to build" and a level (A to E). Every brick and piece of furniture shows a word card: tap the word that names it and it goes in. Read a page at the big moments, and read the ⭐ missions to know what to build next.

For the grown-ups: read-aloud uses this device's own voices, and the microphone and every link to another website ask a grown-up first. The castle facts are real and simplified for play: stone walls and lime mortar, keeps, moats, drawbridges and the portcullis, battlements (merlons and crenels), arrow slits, stained glass, the great hall, tapestries, separate kitchens, spiral stairs, the garderobe, pages, squires and knights, quintains and jousting with blunted lances, destriers and grooms, falconry, jesters, the blacksmith, candles and torches, market days and fireworks. Knights here only train and joust for points; nothing is ever hurt, and the dragon is a story dragon that breathes sparkles. Three castles can be saved on this device. To install on a tablet, host the folder on any web server (or GitHub Pages) and use "Add to Home Screen".
