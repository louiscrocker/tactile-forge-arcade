A copepod's year, from a tiny egg to the deep and back. Colour them in!

1. The life cycle of a copepod

Eleven moults in one year: egg → nauplius I–VI → copepodite I–V (the last is stage V, full of oil) → the long sleep in the deep → grown up → eggs again.

5. Make a plankton net

  • An old pair of tights or a fine stocking
  • A wire coat hanger bent into a ring
  • A small clear jar, tied in the toe
  • A long string

Pull the net slowly through the water, then look in the jar with a magnifying glass. Little jumping specks? Copepods!

⚠️ Only near water with a grown-up, from a dock or a beach. Put every living thing back into the water afterwards.
