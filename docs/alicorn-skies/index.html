How to play

1

Walk and gallop

Arrow keys or WASD walk. Keep holding and you gallop. Tap ↑ to jump. Hold ↓ to sit down (at night you fall asleep!).

2

Fly

Hold Space (or the FLY button) to flap up. Let go to glide. ↓ dives. You can land on the ground, on clouds, on the water and even on the Moon.

3

Horn magic

E (or MAGIC) makes a sparkle. It wakes sleepy flowers, lights crystals, lifts fallen stars and cheers up grumpy clouds. Near a friend, E talks to them instead.

4

Rainbow dash

Shift (or DASH) zooms you forward with a rainbow trail. It uses the sparkle meter, which fills up as you catch stars.

5

Quests

Friends with a ! have a quest for you. Follow the big arrow. The quest card at the top says what to do; tap 🔊 to hear it.

6

Stars and the wardrobe

Fly through floating stars to collect them, then spend them in the wardrobe on bows, crowns, scarves and sparkles. Some things are prizes for quests.

7

The magic map

Press M to see the whole land. Tap a place on the map and you fly straight there.

8

Your baby alicorn

Find the lost foal in the woods and take her home to the stable. Press E next to her (or tap her) to feed her apples, brush her, play and dress her up. Care makes her grow — when she's grown up she flies with you!

9

Swim, skate, race

Stand on the lake and hold ↓ to dive and swim with the fish. In winter the lake freezes — slide on the ice! Walk under the rainbow arch to race Ember.

10

Games with friends

A friend with a ★ wants to play: count apples with Bramble, spell words with Professor Hoot.

11

Two players

Turn on Player 2 in settings. They use IJKL to move, U to fly, O for magic and H to dash, or a second gamepad.

12

Read to play

In Settings, choose I read to play and a level A to E. The MAGIC, FLY and DASH buttons show words to read first, story pages come after each quest, and written missions pop up. Tap any word to hear it.

For the grown-ups: the "Did you know?" cards are real science (rainbows, clouds, stars, the Moon, horses, owls, frogs, butterflies…) in kid-sized pieces, each with a link to see the real thing. Nothing in the game can hurt the alicorn; the worst that happens is a grumpy cloud blows you sideways. To install on a tablet, host the folder on any web server (or GitHub Pages) and use "Add to Home Screen".
