      How to play

      1

      Hatch

      Press Space (or tap GO!) again and again to wriggle out of your jelly egg.

      2

      Swim and scrape

      Arrow keys or WASD swim. Find the green fuzzy algae on rocks and weeds and hold Space next to it to scrape it off. You can also tap the water to swim there.

      3

      Watch the bottom

      Dragonfly nymphs lurk on the mud. When one twitches, tap Space for a burst of speed and get away.

      4

      Grow legs

      Eat enough and your back legs sprout. Now you can eat the wrigglers hanging from the surface too: sneak up slowly. Eat more and your front legs pop out.

      5

      Breathe

      With front legs you have lungs. Swim ↑ to the surface to gulp air; your tail shrinks faster while you breathe. When it is gone, you are a froglet.

      6

      Hop and snap

      Swim into a lily pad or the bank to climb out. ← → hop, ↑ jumps high. When a bug glows, press Space to flick your tongue.

      7

      Heron!

      When the heron wades in, dive deep, hide under a lily pad, or hold ↓ on a pad to stay still as a stone. It only gives you a fright: nothing worse ever happens.

      8

      Sing, spawn, sleep

      As a frog, press E to sing and the chorus answers. Eat until you are full, then press E at the glowing weed to lay eggs. In winter, dive to the glowing mud and press Space to sleep under the ice until spring.
