POND HUNT

Take this to a real pond in spring or summer. Look, do not grab. Tick what you find.

🥚Frog spawn

A clear jelly clump with black dots, floating near the edge in the shallows. Toad spawn comes in long strings instead.

🐸Tadpoles

Black commas with wriggly tails, often in a crowd in warm shallow water. Watch one scrape a rock.

🦵A tadpole with legs

Look for two tiny back legs first. Front legs come later and pop out all at once.

🐸A froglet

A frog smaller than your thumbnail, maybe with a stub of tail, sitting at the very edge.

🌿Algae

Green fuzz on rocks, sticks or the side of the pond. Tadpole food. Slippery!

🦟Wrigglers

Mosquito larvae hanging upside-down from the surface of still water. Tap the water and watch them dive.

🐉A dragonfly nymph or its husk

A brown six-legged bug on the bottom, or an empty split skin still clinging to a reed after the dragonfly flew away.

✨A dragonfly or damselfly

Dragonflies rest with wings out flat; damselflies fold theirs back.

🍃A lily pad

Touch the top (waxy, dry) and look for the notch. Is anything sitting on it, or hiding under it?

🎵A frog call

Sit still at dusk and listen. Banjo twang? Peep? Snore? Can you count how many are singing?

🐦A heron

A tall grey bird standing perfectly still in the shallows, or flying with its neck folded in an S.

🐌A pond snail

On a stone or a weed, grazing algae just like a tadpole does.

🐟Minnows

A flash of silver in a school. Do they scatter when your shadow falls on the water?

🌾Cattails or reeds

Tall stalks at the edge, with brown sausage heads in late summer. A great place for nymphs to climb out.

💧Rings on the water

A raindrop, a rising fish, or a frog diving. What made the ring you saw?

🧊Winter: ice on top

Come back in winter. Is the pond frozen on top? The frogs are asleep in the mud underneath.

Pond rules: a grown-up comes too · look with your eyes and a jar, not your hands · put everything back where you found it · wash your hands after · never take spawn or tadpoles home unless a grown-up says the pond needs it.
