    How to play

    1

    Hatch

    Press Space (or tap GO!) again and again to chew out of your egg.

    2

    Crawl and eat

    Arrow keys or WASD move you along the stem. At a fork, hold the arrow pointing toward the leaf you want, or click anywhere on the plant to walk there. On a leaf, hold Space to bite. When a leaf is eaten up, find another.

    3

    Molt five times

    Eat enough and you shed your skin. Monarch caterpillars have five instars. Ants shove, a wasp hunts and a mantis waits on one leaf: hold ↓ to freeze, sit still on a leaf, or drop on a silk line and climb back.

    4

    Hang the J, become a chrysalis

    When the 5th instar is full, find a glowing spot and press Space. You hang in a J, shed one last skin, and become a jade chrysalis with gold dots. Press Space to twitch.

    5

    Emerge, drink, pop the pods

    Hang while your wings unfold and dry. Then Space to fly, steer with the arrows, and press Space at the pink milkweed flowers to drink nectar. Press Space at a ripe seed pod to burst it and collect seeds for your garden.

    6

    Fly south

    The map scrolls: fly to the right. Space or ↑ flaps (costs energy); let go to glide; ↓ dives (fast, shakes off birds). Ride thermals up. Fly high in a tailwind, low in a headwind. Press Space at flowers to drink, at trees to roost for the night or shelter from a storm. At the roost, read tomorrow’s forecast and choose to fly at dawn or wait a day.

    7

    Mexico, winter, and the relay north

    Reach the fir forest and press Space at a fir to join the cluster. Sleep through winter, plant your seeds, fly north to Texas, and live a short summer life: drink, then lay eggs (female) or find a mate (male). Three generations later you are back in Ontario, and a new super generation is born.
