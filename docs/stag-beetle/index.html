    How to play

    1

    Hatch

    Wait, then push the arrows to wiggle out of your egg.

    2

    Eat the stump

    Push into soft wood to chew it. White fungus wood is the best. Dark oak is too hard, for now: the stump softens every year.

    3

    The years go by

    Watch the garden above you. Each year something new happens. In winter, dig below the white frost line and sleep. Press E to sing.

    4

    Make your cocoon

    From Year 3, at the end of summer, you can dig into the soil and press Space. Or stay and grow: eating more means bigger antlers.

    5

    Fly at dusk

    Press E to warm up your wings. Hold ↑ to stay up, and follow the gold puffs. You are heavy! Land too hard and press Space to rock over.

    6

    The Antler Ring

    Space pushes. Orange ring: press Space as it closes, to block. Green: press E to hook, then Space to lift and flip. Red: a trick. Wait!

    7

    The sneaky way

    A small beetle can creep under the branch while the big ones wrestle. Stop when one looks!

    8

    Her way

    A girl bites the bark to make the sap run, smells out the right wood, digs down and lays her eggs beside it.
