The lobsterman's gauge: in Maine, a lobster whose head-shield is shorter than 8.3 cm must go straight back into the sea. This long:

For grown-ups: American lobsters live along the Atlantic coast of North America, from Labrador to North Carolina, most of all in the Gulf of Maine. Their relatives (the European lobster, crayfish, crabs and shrimps) live on almost every coast and in many rivers. Turn rocks back gently the way you found them, keep animals in water if you look at them, and put them back where they came from. Lobster claws pinch hard: let a grown-up handle any lobster or crab.
