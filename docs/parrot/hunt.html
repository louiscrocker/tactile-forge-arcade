🦜 Bird watching

You do not need a rainforest. There are wild birds in every yard, park and street. Go and watch them with a grown-up. Take this sheet and a pencil.

LOOK FROM FAR AWAY. BE QUIET. WILD BIRDS STAY WILD.

The bird watcher's rules

  1. I keep still and I keep quiet. Then the birds come closer by themselves.
  2. I look with my eyes (or binoculars), not with my hands.
  3. I never chase a bird, and I never touch a nest or an egg.
  4. If I find a baby bird on the ground, I leave it and tell a grown-up. Its parents are nearly always close by.
  5. A wild bird belongs outside, with its flock.
