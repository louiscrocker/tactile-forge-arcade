How to Play

A short briefing before you cast off.

🌬️

Wind & Sail

Your speed depends on the angle between your heading and the wind. Broad reach is fastest, in irons is dead stop.

🎲

The Turn

Pick a heading on the chart, roll 2d6, then move that many spaces — modified by your point of sail.

🚩

Marks & Laps

Round each mark in order on the correct side. Complete all laps and cross the finish to win.

⚓

Tack & Jibe

Crossing the wind costs crew stamina and may fail. Plan your heading changes.

🃏

Event Cards

Squalls, dolphins, broken halyards — fortune favors the prepared skipper.

🛡️

Right of Way

Starboard tack has priority. Collisions earn a penalty turn for the violator.
