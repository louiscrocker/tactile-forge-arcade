My best find:
A close-up (use a magnifying glass):
A baby ephyra is only a few millimetres across. A grown moon jelly can be as wide as a dinner plate. This ruler is 8 cm:

For grown-ups: moon jellies (Aurelia aurita and its close relatives) live in coastal seas all over the world, often in quiet bays and harbours, and sometimes gather in great summer blooms. Their sting is very weak, but other jellies can sting badly, and stranded jellies can still sting: look, take photos, and never touch. A polyp is only a few millimetres tall; look for them with a magnifying glass on the shady undersides of docks and rocks.
