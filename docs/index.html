Play anywhere

Every game runs in a modern browser on a laptop or tablet. Nothing to install. The arcade games look best with WebGL; they fall back to a simpler renderer if your device doesn't have it.

Nothing tracked

No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no cookies. These pages load nothing from any other website. Progress and high scores are saved only in your own browser.

Made for families

The nature games are written for young readers, with real facts about the animals and weather they explore and everything read aloud.

For grown-ups

Read-aloud and the optional microphone (“I read to play”) use your browser's built-in speech features. In Chrome these can send the spoken text or the child's voice to Google; the microphone is off unless you turn it on. Fact cards include “See the real thing” links that open YouTube or Wikipedia in a new tab.

Tell us what you think

Found a bug, got an idea, or your kids loved something? Grown-ups can send feedback through a short form on GitHub (a free GitHub account is needed). Every note gets read.
