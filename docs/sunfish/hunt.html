A hatchling sunfish is about 2 mm long. A grown-up can be 2 metres long and taller than that from fin tip to fin tip, and weigh as much as a car: about sixty million times heavier than when it hatched!

For grown-ups: the ocean sunfish (Mola mola) lives in temperate and tropical seas worldwide and visits Monterey Bay and the California coast in summer and autumn. Look at sunfish and jellies at an aquarium, never touch a jelly, and keep well back from wild animals. The game's life cycle is simplified: little is known about where sunfish spawn.
