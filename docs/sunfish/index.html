    SUNFISHSUN AND SEAFrom a tiny star to a giant

    Be an ocean sunfish, the heaviest bony fish in the sea! Hatch from one of three hundred million eggs, grow spikes like a tiny star, swim in a school, then dive deep into the cold dark for jellies and lie flat in the sun to warm up. Gulls and little fish pick off your itchy sea lice, sea lions and orcas swim by, and you grow sixty million times heavier… then swim back to the warm sea to start the next generation.

    How to play

    1

    Hatch

    You start as one of three hundred million tiny eggs, drifting in the warm open ocean. When your egg is ready, push any arrow to wiggle out.

    2

    A hatchling in the plankton

    Swim with the arrows and bump into copepods and krill to eat them. Blue ones glow at night. Plankton is near the top at night and deeper by day. Press E to dash.

    3

    A spiky star

    Eat and you grow spikes all over, like a tiny star! Anchovies, comb jellies and gulls come by: they never hurt you, at most you tumble. E puts your spikes out and dashes.

    4

    A young sunfish

    Your spikes fade and a frilly rudder (the clavus) grows where a tail would be. Swim with your school near the top and eat krill and little salps. A fast fish? Stay with your friends or dash.

    5

    Dive for jellies

    A real sunfish now! Dive deep, swim up to a jelly and press Space to slurp it. Watch the 🌡️ warm meter: the deep is cold. At the top, press Space to lie flat in the sun and warm up. A gull may land and peck your sea lice off.

    6

    Itchy? Get clean!

    When you are itchy (🐛), follow the arrow to the cleaning station in the kelp, by the shore. Press Space and hold still: little señoritas and halfmoon fish pick you clean. Later, swim up fast and press E at the top to jump right out of the sea!

    7

    Visitors

    Sea lions, orcas and blue sharks come for a look: a big flap (E) and they swim off. A boat slows down for your fin. If you swim into a fishing net, the fishers lift you out and let you go.

    8

    The warm sea

    A giant at last! Follow the arrow back out to the warm sea. Find a sunfish with hearts and press Space to say hi. Boys let a little milky cloud go beside her. Girls press Space each time the glow swells to let their eggs go: three hundred million of them!

    For the grown-ups: read-aloud uses this device's own voices, and the microphone and every link to another website ask a grown-up first. The ocean sunfish's life cycle is real, simplified for play: hundreds of millions of tiny eggs let go in warm open ocean; see-through hatchlings in the plankton; the spiky, star-shaped larva (sunfish are cousins of pufferfish); the young sunfish losing its spikes as the clavus forms from the top and bottom fins, swimming in small schools; grown sunfish flapping their tall fins like wings, diving hundreds of metres to eat jellies, salps and comb jellies, and lying flat at the surface, thought to warm up after cold dives; parasites, gulls pecking them off, cleaning stations in California's kelp forests with señoritas and halfmoon fish, breaching; sea lions, orcas and big sharks as the few animals that bother a big sunfish; boats and nets (careful fishers let sunfish go); growth of about sixty million times in weight. Nothing in the game is ever hurt. Little is known about where sunfish spawn: the warm sea is a simplification.
