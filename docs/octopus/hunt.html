A newly hatched octopus is as small as a grain of rice (6 mm). A settler is about as big as a pea. A giant Pacific octopus can stretch its arms four metres: about two grown-ups lying head to toe! This long is a settler's arm span:

For grown-ups: giant Pacific octopuses live all around the North Pacific, from Puget Sound and British Columbia to Alaska, Japan and Korea, from the shallows down to the deep. Smaller octopuses live on rocky shores all over the world. Turn rocks back gently the way you found them, keep animals in water if you look at them, and put them back where they came from. An octopus's beak can bite: let a grown-up handle any octopus or crab.
