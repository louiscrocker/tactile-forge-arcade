Ant HuntTake the game outside

Find each of these in a real garden, park or pavement. Crouch down low and be patient: ants are busy, and they will show you where they are going. Tick the box when you spot one. Look, don't touch: some ants sting!

Explorer's name:

An ant hillA little heap of fine soil with a hole in the middle. Every grain was carried up by an ant!
An ant trailAnts walking one behind another along the same path. They are following a smell trail!
An ant carrying somethingA seed, a crumb or a bit of bug. Is it bigger than the ant? Where is she taking it?
Ants working togetherTwo or more ants pulling the same big thing. Watch how they turn it to get it home.
Ants with aphidsAnts walking up a stem to a group of aphids. Watch an ant tap an aphid with its feelers for a sweet drop!
A ladybug larvaThe spiky little alligator that eats aphids. Are any ants chasing it away?
An ant with wingsOn a warm summer day, winged queens and males fly out. A queen that has landed may be looking for a place to dig.
A hole in the groundA tiny round hole with ants going in and out. Or maybe an earthworm hole after rain?
Two kinds of soilFind sandy soil and sticky clay soil. Which one holds a shape when you squeeze it? Which lets water through?
A seed with an ant snackSome seeds (like violets and bloodroot) have a pale little lump that ants love. Can you find one?
Experiment: put a tiny crumb of cookie near an ant trail and wait. How long before the first ant finds it? How many come after that? That is recruitment: the first ant went home and told her sisters with a smell trail.
Be kind: ants are part of the garden. Watch them, don't squash them, and wash your hands after digging.
