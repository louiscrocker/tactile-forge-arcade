    How to play

    1

    Into the sea

    You start as one of thousands of eggs under your mum, at the edge of the sea before dawn. When a wave comes, push any arrow to wiggle into the water. You hatch the moment you touch it!

    2

    A zoea at sea

    Swim with the arrows and bump into plankton to eat it. Green diatoms are food, blue ones glow at night, and copepods jump away: chase them! Press E to flick your tail and dash. Plankton is near the top at night and deep down by day. Eat to moult: zoea I, II, III.

    3

    Visitors

    A school of fish, a whale shark, a manta ray… They never hurt you: if they get too close you just tumble away. Swim clear of the whale shark's big mouth!

    4

    The megalopa

    After your last moult you have claws and a tail. Follow the arrow back to the island. Next to a jellyfish, press Space to ride it home. The waves carry you onto the rocks.

    5

    March up the cliff

    Now you are a tiny crab. Walk sideways up the shore and climb the cliff to the rainforest. Watch your gills (💧): the hot sun dries them, so rest under rocks and in cracks. Hide when a bird swoops!

    6

    Grow up in the forest

    Press Space by fallen leaves, flowers, figs and seedlings to eat them. When you are full, push down on soft soil to dig a burrow. Deep enough? Press Space to shut the door with leaves and moult. Wait out the dry season, then dig up and out, bigger! Hide in your burrow if the robber crab comes.

    7

    The great march

    When you are grown up, wait for the first big rain. Then march to the sea with millions of other crabs: over the crab bridge, down the cliff, and press Space at the water for a dip.

    8

    Boy or girl

    Boys dig a burrow on the shore terrace and push rivals away: tap Space to push, and press E (or ▲) when the ring turns green for a big shove. Girls find a boy's burrow, keep their eggs safe for twelve days, then go to the sea's edge before dawn and press Space each time a wave washes over them to shake the eggs into the sea.

    For the grown-ups: read-aloud uses this device's own voices, and the microphone and every link to another website ask a grown-up first. The Christmas Island red crab's life cycle is real, simplified for play: eggs released into the sea at the turn of the high tide before dawn in the last quarter of the moon; eggs that hatch on contact with sea water; planktonic zoea larvae that moult several times over about a month (shown as three); plankton that rise at night and sink by day; whale sharks and manta rays that visit at spawning time; the megalopa stage returning to the shore; tiny crabs about 5 mm across marching up to the plateau; gills that must stay damp; leaf-litter, flower, fruit and seedling feeding that shapes the forest; solitary burrows plugged with leaves in the dry season; several moults a year while young, over four to five years (one moult per stage in the game); robber (coconut) crabs; the first wet-season rains starting the migration; road closures, crab fences and the crab bridge built by Parks Australia rangers; yellow crazy ants as a threat; males digging burrows on the shore terrace and fighting over them; females brooding about 100,000 eggs for about twelve days. Nothing in the game is ever hurt. To install on a tablet, host the folder on any web server (or GitHub Pages) and use "Add to Home Screen".
