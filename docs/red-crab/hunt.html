For grown-ups: Christmas Island red crabs live only on Christmas Island (and the nearby Cocos Islands) in the Indian Ocean, but crabs and their relatives live on almost every coast. Turn rocks back gently the way you found them, keep animals in water if you look at them, and put them back where they came from. Never touch jellyfish, even ones washed up on the beach.
