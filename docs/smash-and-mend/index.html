    How to play

    🔨

    Smash with Mungo

    Walk with ← →. Punch with X (or J, F). Hold ↑ while punching to punch up, ↓ to punch down. To climb, walk into the side of the building and hold ↑. Jump with Space or ↑, then press ↓ in the air to STOMP. Bricks with nothing under them fall and crumble. Smash from the top down to keep the dust off the neighbours.

    🔧

    Mend with Pip

    Climb the drainpipes with ↑ ↓ and step off onto the scaffold with ← →. Things that need fixing glow and pulse. Stand next to one and press X to fix it. Hold ↓ to lift your bucket and catch falling things.

    😵

    Bumps

    Nobody ever gets hurt. A bump makes you dizzy for a moment, then you carry on.

    👪 For grown-ups

    What it is. A gentle arcade game for about ages 5 to 8: two kinds of level along one town street. In SMASH levels Mungo, a demolition-crew giant, knocks down old buildings that the town has emptied for rebuilding. In MEND levels Pip, a small repair kid, climbs scaffolding and fixes a family's house while catching things that fall. A two-player mode puts both on one screen.

    Kind by design. Nobody is ever hurt. A bump is a cartoon wobble with dizzy stars and play goes straight on. There are no lives and no game over; on Super crew the clock can run out, and you simply try again. Buildings are always empty before they are knocked down (the sign says "ALL OUT!"), and the pigeon and cat napping in two windows hop out safely as soon as Mungo knocks.

    Difficulty. Little helper has no clock, slow falling things, one-tap fixes and shadows showing where things land. Big helper adds a clock that only affects the stars. Super crew has tougher steel, three-tap fixes, no landing shadows and a real time limit.

    Privacy. Nothing leaves this device. Progress is kept in this browser's storage. Read-aloud uses the device's own voices. There are no adverts, accounts, purchases or links out.

    Controls. Keyboard, touch buttons (shown on touch screens) and up to two gamepads.
