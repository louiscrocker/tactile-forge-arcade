A new sea star is smaller than a pinhead. A big ochre sea star is about 25 cm across, as wide as a dinner plate. This long is a young sea star, arm tip to arm tip:

For grown-ups: ochre sea stars live on rocky shores from Alaska to Baja California. Go at low tide (the lowest tides come with the full and new moon), watch the waves, and never turn your back on the sea. Touch gently with one wet finger, never pull a sea star off a rock, turn rocks back the way you found them, and put every animal back where it came from.
