How long is a big Hercules beetle? About 17 cm, this long:

For grown-ups: many beetle larvae (grubs) live in rotting logs and are a vital part of how forests recycle wood. Roll logs back exactly as you found them, handle animals gently and briefly (or not at all), and wash hands afterwards. Hercules beetles live in Central and South America and the Caribbean; wherever you are, rhinoceros beetles, stag beetles or other wood-eating beetles are worth looking for.
