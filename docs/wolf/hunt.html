For grown-ups: gray wolves live across North America, Europe and Asia; this game is set in a Yellowstone-style valley, where wolves returned in 1995 and 1996. You are unlikely to meet a wolf on a walk, but the same tracking skills work for dogs, foxes, deer, birds and squirrels. Look at tracks without touching wild animals or their homes, leave burrows and nests alone, and wash hands after a nature walk.
