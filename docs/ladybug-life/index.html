    How to play

    1

    Hatch

    Press Space (or tap GO!) again and again to wriggle out of your egg.

    2

    Crawl the branches

    Arrow keys or WASD move you along the stem. At a fork, hold the arrow pointing toward the branch you want. You can also click anywhere on the plant to walk there.

    3

    Eat aphids

    Bump into an aphid and you grab it. Mash Space to chew faster. Sneak up quick — scared aphids drop off the plant!

    4

    Molt and grow

    Eat enough and you shed your skin, growing into the next instar. There are four larval instars.

    5

    Watch out

    Ants shove you off their aphids: go round by another branch. When a bird comes, hold ↓ to freeze (or play dead as an adult), or hide under a leaf.

    6

    Pupate

    When the 4th instar is full, find a glowing leaf spot and press Space. Press Space as a pupa to twitch.

    7

    Fly and explore

    Once your wings dry, Space takes off. Fly to the other plants — each has its own aphids. Land near any branch with Space. Eat more, then press E at a leaf to lay eggs.

    📖

    I read to play

    Pick "I read to play" and a level (A to E). Then Space shows words: tap the one that fits and your ladybug does it. Read a page to grow, and read the ⭐ missions to know where to go. Tap any word to hear it; 1–3 pick a word from the keyboard.

    8

    A whole year

    Seasons turn. In autumn the leaves fall. When winter comes, fly to the warm crack in the garden wall and press Space to hibernate until spring.
