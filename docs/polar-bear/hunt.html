This dashed circle is 3 cm across. A grown polar bear's paw print can be 30 cm wide: ten circles in a row! How many of your hands fit across 30 cm?

For grown-ups: polar bears live around the Arctic Ocean (Canada, Alaska, Greenland, Norway's Svalbard and Russia) and depend on the sea ice. You will not meet one on a walk, but the same noticing skills work everywhere: tracks of dogs, cats and birds, ice and snow, the wind. Look at animal homes without touching them, leave burrows and nests alone, and wash hands after a nature walk. Real polar bears are dangerous to people: they are watched only from safe places like the tundra buggies in the game.
